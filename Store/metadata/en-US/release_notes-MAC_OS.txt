Make the window taller than it is wide and the forecast plays on a television.

• The set. A window in portrait shows the picture on a CRT rather than letterboxing it.
  Its buttons work: settings, back and forward through the rotation, and a power button
  that switches the tube off the way it used to.
• Three finishes. Studio monitor grey, wood veneer, or black plastic. There is also a
  switch to keep the set in a wide window.
• The whole rotation, by default. Hourly Forecast, Travel, the hourly graph and the
  severe-weather outlook used to start switched off. Everything is on now; turn off what
  you do not want in Settings.
• A new app icon and on-screen mark.
