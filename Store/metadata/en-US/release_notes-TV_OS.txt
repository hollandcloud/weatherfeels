• The television now makes the noise a CRT makes. Switching the set on or off sounds like
  the real thing — the flyback whine, the degauss thump, and the anode cracking into the
  tube face — synthesised rather than sampled.

• The set's power button is reachable from the remote at last. It was decorative on Apple
  TV: the focus engine never got to the cabinet, so nothing you pressed could switch the
  television off. Power now sits on the controls overlay as well.

• The cabinet's buttons show which one the remote is on. They used to look exactly the same
  focused or not, so the only way to find out was to press one. A focused button now swells
  and brightens the way a lit control does.

• The Hourly forecast's columns stay inside the blue panel. The hour and the wind speed hung
  off either edge onto the orange field behind them.
