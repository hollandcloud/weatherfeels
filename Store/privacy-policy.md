# Privacy Policy — WeatherStar

_Last updated: 5 August 2026_

WeatherStar does not have accounts, does not have a server, and does not collect
analytics. This policy is short because there is very little to describe.

## What leaves your device

**Your location, to fetch a forecast.** When you follow your device's location or
choose a city, the app sends that coordinate to:

- **api.weather.gov** — the US National Weather Service, to fetch the forecast,
  current conditions, nearby observation stations and any active alerts for that point.
- **mesonet.agron.iastate.edu** — the Iowa Environmental Mesonet at Iowa State
  University, to fetch the radar image covering that region.

These requests are made directly from your device to those services over HTTPS. We
never see them. Both are operated by third parties under their own privacy practices.

**A music folder you point us at.** If you configure a music server of your own, the
app requests files from the address you entered. Nothing else is sent there.

## What stays on your device

- Your chosen city, recent cities, and every setting — stored in the app's own
  preferences on the device.
- Your Apple Music library and playlist selection. Track titles and artwork are read
  on the device to play them and are never transmitted.
- Music files you add to the app.

## What we do not do

- No accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
- No analytics, crash reporting, or usage measurement.
- No advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking across apps or websites.
- No third-party analytics, attribution, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
- No sale or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.

## Location permission

Location access is optional. If you decline it, or revoke it later, the app keeps
working — choose a city by name or ZIP code instead. You can change or revoke the
permission at any time in your device's system settings.

## Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects nothing, so there is nothing to disclose under
COPPA or similar regimes.

## Data retention and rights

We hold no personal data, so there is nothing to request, correct, export, or delete
on our side. Everything the app knows about you lives on your device and is removed
when you delete the app.
